## Operational limits

Attempt authority is process-local and bound to the signed browser identity. Restarting the backend loses pending verification; absent browser storage blocks departure and missing/stale return state gets safe unchanged-bag recovery. Stripe attempts expire after about 31 minutes. Known failure/cancel/expiry cleanup is bounded; lost provider responses, abandonment, restart and cleanup failure rely on short signed-link expiry and the asynchronous one-day bucket lifecycle. Stripe can retain test Products/Sessions/coupons and fetched images under its own retention. Per-session display name does not replace the underlying Stripe account in every Link/legal/receipt surface. No durable webhook fulfilment, connected merchant account or real-payment capability was added.
