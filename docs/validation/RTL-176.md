## Actual change

The session-owned Shopify controller filters explicit `giftCard:true` products after bounded page merging, before publishing catalogue state. CatalogueBrowser derives its cards, search, loaded count, selection/Select all and detail view from that filtered state. A product newly flagged as a gift card replaces and removes its older clothing entry when pages merge. Ordinary clothing named “Gift Cardigan” and older summaries without the optional flag remain visible; no title heuristic is introduced.

Gift-card-only card text/selection branches are now unreachable and removed. An empty filtered page with another cursor explains that Load more can continue; an exhausted collection says no products are available to import. Actual cursors, page/limit metadata and explicit pagination remain unchanged. There are no automatic fill-page requests or changed provider work/rate limits.

The existing backend `catalogue.importProduct` still rejects native `isGiftCard` (and gift-card product type) before calling `importPhotos`. The independent browser `importCandidate` gift-card rejection remains unchanged. This does not remove any merchant's Shopify gift cards or mutate their store. No API/schema/query/provider, credential or CSS change.
