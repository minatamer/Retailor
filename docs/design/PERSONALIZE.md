# Personalize — website messaging

15 October 2026, RTL-067. Owner-selected feature name: **Personalize**. Replace the Storefronts navbar label and carry this concept across the public landing, metadata, creation journey, Tailor guidance and owner entry points. The actual shop remains a storefront; internal IDs, routes and design contracts retain their meaning.

## Promise and real choices

Your store. Your style. Start from a designed look, then personalize the supported choices with Tailor or the same manual controls:

- Studio, Pulse or Gallery as a starting layout; optional independent Home, Collection and Product family choices.
- Five existing merchant palettes and three type presets.
- Store name, headline and available hero-photo/brand fields.
- Keep a wordmark or explicitly explore/review an optional three-logo batch. Choosing a look, typing, refreshing or entering this section does not generate logos.

Keep the photos, products, bag, stock and owner records connected. Preview/confirm and owner Apply/Cancel keep their existing authority. This is a temporary demo with finite supported choices; no arbitrary design editor, guaranteed live AI quality, automatic publishing or real checkout claim.
