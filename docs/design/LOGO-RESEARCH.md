These sources support design constraints, not a claim that an object will improve conversion, be universally understood or be legally unique. The choices/rankings below are Retailor-specific design judgments drawn from the actual product and owner brief.

## Three selected directions

**E — Hanger: the clothes.** A single open hook and triangular shoulders give the R a clothing context. This fits both “retail” and the tailoring association without returning to the rejected thread/fold monograms. The R stays in front of the hanger, instead of becoming a tiny stitched detail. Risk: a hanger alone can suggest laundry/dry cleaning. The full Retailor wordmark and storefront messaging supply the wider context; the16px hook/counters need deliberate inspection.

**F — Storefront: the result.** A striped awning above a solid shop front makes a bold space for a white R. This is my strongest overall direction for the platform: it expresses the result the owner wants to market, “see your store,” instead of centering payment or time spent configuring it. Two broad white stripes keep the silhouette compact. Risk: this is a general retail symbol rather than exclusively clothing; distinct type/proportions and surrounding product imagery carry that specificity.

**G — Receipt: the connected business.** A tall paper silhouette with a clear tear edge, two short lines and a large white R connects shopping with the owner records behind it. It gives the R more uninterrupted area than the barcode. Risk: it may suggest accounting/POS; make the product promise and full name visible in larger contexts. Avoid real amounts, barcode data or decorative microcopy.

| Hanger       | Clothing and styling                  | Can suggest a cleaning service                                                  | Draw E; strongest clothing association in this set. |
| Storefront   | Creating a usable store quickly       | Broad retail association                                                        | Draw F; recommended platform direction to refine.   |
| Receipt      | Shopping plus connected owner records | Accounting association                                                          | Draw G; useful business-side alternative.           |
| Price tag    | Clothing/retail                       | Too close to the rejected label direction; can imply discounts                  | Retain as an unexecuted alternative.                |
| Needle/spool | Tailoring/craft                       | Overstates bespoke garment-making; returns toward the rejected thread direction | Do not advance in this round.                       |
| Parcel       | Commerce/fulfilment                   | Centers shipping rather than creating a store; the demo has no real shipment    | Do not advance in this round.                       |

A–D remain available in navy/white: cart is direct but generic; wallet/card lean toward finance/payments; barcode is retail-oriented but visibly busy at16px. None is silently discarded or selected for the owner. No green remains in any current mark, lockup, PNG study or board.
