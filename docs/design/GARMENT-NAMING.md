# Garment naming and category vocabulary

15 October 2026, RTL-068. The owner chooses word capitalization for default names and familiar ecommerce category labels. New generated titles use Straight-leg Jeans, Wide-leg Jeans, Button-front Shirt or Bootcut Jeans when the silhouette is supported. Add a visible colour/pattern/cut only if it helps distinguish the item. A concise safe title is better than invented detail. Denim as a navigation label is a merchandising convention, not evidence of a garment's fibre/material composition. Do not infer cotton, stretch, selvedge, premium quality, measurements or wearer fit from that label. Preserve the existing recognition evidence restrictions.

Official retailer pages inspected on 15 October 2026:

- [Zara men's jeans](https://www.zara.com/us/en/man-jeans-l659.html): Jeans with Straight / Regular, Slim, Loose, Baggy, Flare and other cut navigation; clothing navigation includes T-Shirts, Shirts, Overshirts, Blazers, Knitwear and Swimwear. Use recognizable product-type terms rather than raw data slugs.
- [Levi's men's bootcut jeans](https://www.levi.com/US/en_US/clothing/men/jeans/bootcut/c/levi_clothing_men_jeans_bootcut): Bootcut and Straight are style/fit subdivisions inside Jeans. Bootcut Jeans remains an item title; do not create one category per fit.
- [UNIQLO men's jeans](https://www.uniqlo.com/us/en/men/bottoms/jeans): Jeans is a dedicated clothing category. Denim is the owner's preferred Retailor display wording for that existing jeans type; it does not imply every retailer uses Denim or broaden the finite classifier to all denim garments.

Distinct types remain distinct rather than sharing labels/counts. Their lower-case IDs are data, never visitor-facing clothing labels. Search should accept both Denim and jeans.
