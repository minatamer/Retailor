## Platform competitors and feasibility

[Shopify's official filter documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/storefront-search/search-and-discovery-filters) distinguishes standard category/price/availability filters from option/metafield-driven custom filters, and describes visual facets and empty-value handling. Retailor already has category, size, known colour, price and availability; the improvement is visible, correctly counted groups and clear recovery. A material/fit/brand/gender filter needs actual confirmed data and is deferred. Displayed USD/EUR budgets retain Retailor's existing exact currency semantics.

[Shopify checkout](https://www.shopify.com/checkout), [Wix ecommerce](https://www.wix.com/ecommerce/website) and [Squarespace commerce](https://www.squarespace.com/ecommerce-website) were reviewed as official platform capability/design sources. Their real account, payment, delivery and tax integrations are not functionality evidence for Retailor. The useful local pattern is a focused checkout with a clear item/total summary and an explicit outcome. No merchant login or platform trial was created.

## Original implementation priorities

1. One animated native-modal bag that opens with the actual selected line, offers bounded quantity controls, and keeps its totals/action usable on a short phone screen.
2. A visibly separate simulated checkout, structured Demo shopper/delivery/payment sections and exact item summary, then a receipt with once-only stock effects and the real owner-record link. No real contact/address/card inputs.
3. Visual category/size/known-colour groups, context-sensitive counts, price controls, availability and a live match/action count. Omit empty colour controls; never infer fabric/fit/measurements from pictures. Preserve current strict public filter shape and restoration/currency fencing.
4. Useful short motion for drawer/step/result/selection/receipt feedback. No delayed money updates, fake progress, scroll hijacking, flashing, infinite decorative animation or animation-suppression setting.
5. Remove repeated implementation/demo paragraphs, the fresh fixture's placeholder product copy and oversized discount text; retain concise truthful simulation, unknown-fact and relevant quote/error information.

These are original decisions informed by several sources, not copies of a single store. [Concrete plan and baseline issues](../design/SHOPPING-REVISION.md) own execution scope. All research artifacts stay ignored; no publication or live-provider acceptance is supplied.
