## Findings and implemented treatment

The photo check was an isolated small spinner with its count mixed into a sentence. The sample-scroll effect explicitly waited for `sampleBusy` to become false, while the target did not exist until the first accepted photo. Visitors therefore missed most of the work. Other asynchronous operations used unrelated rings, dots, bars or plain text; permanent photo failures ranged from tiny text to a second custom icon.

Shared `LoadingState` now provides a warm bordered navy/cream panel, animated stitch mark, clear title/supporting detail and a distinct cancellation area. A determinate track is used only for the photo controller's real checked count. Other work uses indeterminate movement without an invented percentage. `LoadingMark` is the compact button treatment. `PhotoSkeleton`/`SkeletonCards` reserve photographic space without creating selectable fake products. `PhotoImage` preserves one real image element, its dimensions/fit/loading policy and all existing callbacks, adding a warm pending background and load-driven reveal. `PhotoPlaceholder` is static and explicitly labels absent/repair photos.

## Preserved controls and verification rules

No change to photo input validation, ordered worker commits, cached bytes, selection bounds, provider requests, recognition, exact commerce, cancellation generations, session expiry/reset, private diagnostics or external cleanup. Old loader CSS/keyframes are removed only after checking their actual consumers; the authored orbit, photo entrances, tour progress and other motion remain.

Native verification must demonstrate intermediate page positions while loading, then real accepted-photo counts and the settled result. A still screenshot alone cannot prove animated scrolling. Cancel, retry/deduplication and no second completion scroll are checked independently. Existing artificial fixture/development mode is disclosed in evidence; controlled visual previews of shared components cannot establish native provider success. Actual results and remaining checks are recorded in [validation](../validation/RTL-184.md).
