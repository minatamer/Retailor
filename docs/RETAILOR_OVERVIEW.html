05 / Big impact, bounded demo

Useful without
production plumbing.

Yes—CRUD works in browser memory, with bounded recovery during the temporary session. No database is needed for this demo's connected product and business records.

Inside the demo

  • Clothing photos, fresh live AI draft, compact review and Tailor-led styling.
  • Three complete templates, four themes, three type presets; optional three-logo generation.
  • Home, collection, detail, bag and fake orders; USD with automatic EUR display.
  • Product CRUD through dashboard and Tailor; linked history uses archival.
  • Order drafts, fulfilment, discounts, returns, refunds and stock changes.
  • Three coherent profit/loss scenarios with synthetic traffic, costs and expenses tied to your catalogue.
  • Two-hour guest workspace, local recovery, export and reset.

Beyond this first demo

  • Real payments, carriers, Shopify, advertising or analytics APIs.
  • Accounts, hosted database or cross-device persistence.
  • Real customer PII or public merchant-store publishing.
  • Freeform page building, colour picking or uploaded fonts.
  • Bookkeeping, tax filings or supplier systems.
  • Unsupported fit or guaranteed-conversion claims.

06 / The proven backbone

The same foundation.
A new experience.

ClipBuilder, ScreenPilot and Retrosum provide the architecture and delivery discipline. Retrosum is the closest reference for smooth setup, exact charts and assistant-to-form interactions.

One browser workspace owns products, accepted design, fictional activity and Tailor conversations. A shared commerce engine owns calculations and reviewed changes.

The backend handles live catalogue recognition, typed Tailor and optional logo requests. Chat buttons, browsing and photo-backed manual product operations work without another model call. A fixed reference-data adapter converts USD storefront prices into EUR. Development starts with deterministic fakes and paid calls disabled.
